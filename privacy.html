Harold privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Personal use

Harold is a self-hosted assistant operated for its owner's personal use. This notice describes its Gmail and Google Sheets connection.

Google access and purpose

The owner authorizes Gmail read-only access and Google Sheets access. Gmail permission allows reading message content and metadata; it does not authorize sending or deleting mail. Google Sheets permission allows reading and editing spreadsheets accessible to the connected account.

Google data is used to carry out the owner's assistant requests. The planned expense feature will extract transaction information from bank alerts, organize ledger entries, and support corrections. Successful bank transactions, merchant or payee descriptions, dates, amounts, account or card identifiers, and references may be involved.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored in Harold's private server profile with restricted file permissions. Retrieved content may appear in local assistant session records. Expense entries may be stored in the owner's selected Google spreadsheet.

When a request involves AI assistance, relevant content may be processed by Harold's configured AI provider. Requested messages or summaries may be delivered through Telegram. Those services have their own privacy and retention policies. Before automated email processing is enabled, the operator must confirm the configured provider's data-use and retention settings are appropriate for Google API data.

The public informational website does not receive OAuth credentials or serve as the OAuth callback. Website hosting is separate from the private assistant. GitHub Pages may log website visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Use and disclosure

Harold's Google connection is for the owner's requested personal-assistant features. Google data is not sold or used for advertising by this application. Any processing must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. These pages do not make claims about third-party AI training or retention settings that have not been verified.

Retention and control

The owner controls the self-hosted assistant and can delete local session records and credentials. Google Sheets entries remain until the owner removes them. Data already processed or delivered by third-party services is subject to those services' retention and deletion controls.

The owner can revoke access in their Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorized Google API access; it does not automatically delete existing local records or spreadsheet entries.

Contact

The owner can manage the integration and raise questions through their existing private Harold conversation.

Harold

Harold is a self-hosted personal assistant built on Hermes Agent. Its owner uses a private Telegram conversation to request assistance.

The Google connection allows Harold to read Gmail messages and access Google Sheets in response to the owner's requests. The planned expense-tracking feature will use bank transaction alerts to help maintain the owner's expense ledger and request clarification through Telegram when needed. Automated expense capture is not yet enabled.

Gmail access is read-only. Google Sheets access permits reading and editing spreadsheets; expense tracking is intended to work with the ledger selected by the owner. Google's permission grants are broader than one email sender or one spreadsheet.

This website provides information about the personal-use Google integration. It does not offer account registration or display mailbox contents, expense records, or connection credentials.

Read the Privacy policy for details about Google data access and handling.
